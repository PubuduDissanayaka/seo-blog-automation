# Performance Monitoring, Refresh, Backlinks and Reporting

The loop does not stop at publish. This is the continuous-improvement half of the system.

## Search Console monitoring

Monitor: clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, queries, landing pages, country, device, search
appearance, indexing, sitemap status, URL inspection where available. Identify: new ranking keywords,
declining pages, high-impression low-CTR pages, keywords moving toward page one, traffic losses,
unindexed content, opportunities.

Average position is an average across queries/users, never an exact fixed rank - say so in reports. Keep
Search Console data clearly separate from GA4 data.

## GA4 monitoring

Organic sessions, engaged sessions, landing pages, engagement rate, conversion events, returning visitors,
geographic and device performance, referral performance. Analytics measures behaviour; ranking measures
search performance - never merge the two.

## Content performance analysis

Evaluate each article against its purpose, not by traffic alone. Consider impressions, clicks, position,
CTR, engagement, conversion, internal-link contribution, topical-authority contribution, freshness,
ranking opportunities. Recommended actions:

- Keep unchanged (a successful article is not changed without evidence).
- Improve title or metadata (high-impression low-CTR).
- Expand missing information.
- Improve internal linking.
- Update outdated information.
- Consolidate overlapping articles (redirect redundant content).
- Investigate a technical issue.

Never recommend changing a page that is already performing, without evidence.

## Content decay and refresh

Recurring process to find outdated or declining articles by evidence: performance trends, outdated facts,
broken links, changing intent, new competitor content, missing info, product/service changes, seasonality.
Refresh from evidence; never bump a publication date without substantive change. Use the Refresh Queue
(sheet 8) to track candidates and the assigned work.

## Backlinks and digital PR

Legitimate only: digital PR, original research, useful resources, industry contributions, relevant guest
contributions, natural link earning, legitimate broken-link opportunities, resource-page outreach,
relevant partnerships. Quality and relevance over quantity.

Never: purchased links, link farms, spam comments, automated backlink spam, PBNs, unrelated directories,
mass low-quality guest posts.

## Monthly report

Sections: executive summary · overall performance · Search Console · GA4 · publishing performance ·
keyword movements · winning articles · declining articles · new keyword opportunities · technical SEO
issues · content refresh priorities · backlink activities · AdSense performance (where available) ·
achievements · unresolved issues · next month's priorities.

Compare equivalent reporting periods; do not draw conclusions from incomplete data; label estimated vs
actual data; never put earnings projections in without their assumptions and limits.

## 30/60/90-day roadmap

Per period: objectives, main activities, target deliverables, dependencies, required agents, budget,
KPIs, completion status, risks. Targets must be realistic and adjustable against the site's baseline and
resources. Never guarantee rankings or revenue.
