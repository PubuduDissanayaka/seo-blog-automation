# Article Brief - <Article ID>

Fill every field before any prose is written. Delete the italic guidance as you fill it.

- **article_id**: *stable unique id, e.g. A-014*
- **title**: *proposed title, <= 60 chars, primary keyword near the front*
- **primary_keyword**: *exactly 1*
- **secondary_keywords**: *exactly 5*
- **search_intent**: *informational | commercial | transactional | navigational | local | mixed*
- **audience**: *who, expertise level*
- **region**: *target country/countries*
- **purpose**: *what the reader gets*
- **questions**: *the questions the article must answer, one per line*
- **research**: *facts to verify + where, with the date each is checked*
- **sources**: *authoritative primary sources to cite (gov, academic, official)*
- **competitor_observations**: *what the SERP winners do, and their gaps*
- **structure**: *H2/H3 skeleton in order, with the keyword placement plan*
- **headings**: *which heading carries the primary, which carries each secondary*
- **internal_links**: *existing URLs to link to (verify each exists first)*
- **external_links**: *3 dofollow authoritative sources, attached to the section that cites them*
- **images**: *subject + alt text per image (>= 4 planned)*
- **metadata**: *title + description + canonical*
- **schema**: *BlogPosting; FAQPage only where a real FAQ is shown*
- **editorial**: *E-E-A-T notes, disclosures, YMYL review requirement*
- **fact_check**: *the facts and the date each was checked*
- **word_count**: *from actual content requirements, never an artificial quota*
- **priority**: *from the scoring model, with the inputs*
